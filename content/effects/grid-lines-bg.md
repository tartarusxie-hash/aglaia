## 它是怎么工作的（中文）

两个 linear-gradient（横线、竖线）叠加平铺，线宽 1px、间距 24–32px。想要「蓝图感」可以把线色压得非常淡，再在中心加一团径向高光。注意内容区的对比度：网格只是垫底，正文永远优先。

## How it works (English)

Stack two linear-gradients (horizontal and vertical lines), 1px wide at 24–32px intervals. For a blueprint feel keep lines very faint and add a soft radial highlight at the center. Watch content contrast: the grid is a backdrop, body text always wins.
